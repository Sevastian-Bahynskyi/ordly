# Catalog additions use headwords and preserve forms

A learner may encounter any inflected form when choosing a word to save. Resolve it against the catalog and add the headword/lemma as the normal Material entry, populated with its catalog data and all verified forms, including the encountered form, rather than creating a standalone entry for that inflection.

This keeps different forms of the same lexical word together and prevents the capture surface from fragmenting the learner's library. Preserve distinct lexical readings, existing duplicate protections, and Review history; the rule does not authorise guessing ambiguous lemmas, inventing forms, or automatically merging existing entries. It applies to word additions, not to reducing whole phrases or sentences to headwords.
